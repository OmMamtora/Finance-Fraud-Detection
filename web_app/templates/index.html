Transaction Intelligence Guide

Explanation of fraud detection indicators, behavioral patterns, and transaction risk signals used by the AI engine.

Fraud Detection Parameters

  • Amount (₹) → Total transaction value being processed.
  • Sender Account → Source account initiating the transaction.
  • Receiver Account → Destination account receiving the funds.
  • Transaction Type → Transaction category such as payment, withdrawal, or transfer.
  • Merchant Category → Business domain associated with the transaction.
  • Location → Geographic origin of transaction activity. Foreign or unknown locations may increase risk.
  • Device Used → Device or access channel used during transaction.
  • Payment Channel → Payment medium including UPI, wallet, card, or banking systems.
  • Transaction Hour → Transaction execution time. Late-night activity may indicate suspicious behavior.
  • Day of Week → Weekly behavioral activity pattern indicator.
  • Mins Since Last Txn → Time interval from previous customer transaction. Very small intervals may indicate automated fraud.
  • Spending Deviation → Difference between current transaction amount and normal customer spending behavior.
  • Velocity Score → Measures transaction frequency and behavioral speed. Higher scores indicate suspicious rapid activity.
  • Geo Anomaly (0–1) → Geographic anomaly risk score where values closer to 1 indicate higher fraud risk.
